## 📧 What We Need

To enable the website's email functionality, we require access to an **official Masjid Al-Falah email account**. This email will be used to send automated notifications for:

### Automated Emails Include:

- ✅ **Donation Receipts** - Sent to donors after successful donations
- ✅ **Event/Lecture Request Notifications** - Sent to admin when someone requests an event
- ✅ **Service Request Notifications** - Sent to admin for service inquiries
- ✅ **Question Submissions** - Sent to admin when visitors ask questions
- ✅ **Admin Notifications** - Updates about donations and activities

---

## 🎯 Recommended Email Setup

### Option 1: Gmail Account (Recommended for Start)

**Best for:** Quick setup, reliable delivery, free up to 500 emails/day

**What we need:**

1. **Email Address:** (e.g., `info@masjid-al-falah.org` or `noreply@masjid-al-falah.org`)
2. **Gmail App Password:** A 16-character password generated from Google Account settings

**Setup Steps for Admin:**

1. Create/use a Gmail account for the mosque
2. Enable 2-Factor Authentication on the account
3. Generate an "App Password" at: https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
4. Provide us with the email and app password

---

### Option 2: Professional Email Service (Recommended for Long-term)

**Best for:** Better deliverability, professional appearance

**Options:**

- **Brevo (formerly Sendinblue)** - Free 300 emails/day
- **Resend** - Free 100 emails/day
- **Custom Domain Email** - If mosque has its own domain
